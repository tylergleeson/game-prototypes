## 1. Identity & numbers — C1

Ratings: us 4.27★/89 [1], gb 4.13★/15 [2], om 0/0 [3], ca 4.13★/15 [4], au 4.33★/6 [5] — thin outside the US. **Renamed** since the brief was written, from "Block Jam NO TIMER" to "Block Game NO TIMER" (subtitle "NO TIMER Color Block Game") [1][6]; old and new URL slugs both resolve to the same id [15]. IAP: 10 SKUs, Coins Bundles and "Item Sets," $1.99–$34.99 [6]. Developer runs ~35 App Store apps [12], almost all unrelated (AI tools, ASMR simulators, coloring, a dating assistant) — this is their only puzzle title, one SKU in a generic app-factory portfolio, not a genre specialist.

## 2. Core rules stated exactly — C2/C3
Listing promises matching blocks "to the same color gate" and clearing the board [1] — the description itself still says "clear all blocks **on time**," leftover timer language in a NO-TIMER game [1]. Screenshots show colored gate tabs on the board edge, consistent with the exit-through-gate family [17]. The real gate is **moves**: the HUD shows "Moves 30" at both Level 40 and Level 47 [17], suggesting a flat/coarse budget rather than a per-level computed par. Half of all 18 reviews (9/18) mention "no timer," and most of those pivot straight to the move limit as the real problem (quotes in §12). Lives: unmentioned in any review — C0 on whether one exists.

## 7. Economy & boosters — C1/C3
IAP blends currency ("Coins Bundle") and boosters ("Item Set"), $1.99–$34.99 [6] (§1). Screenshots show two-plus soft currencies and a booster row beside undo [17]; a "+250" coin burst plays on every level clear [17] — celebratory, but not tied to any observed purchase moment, so it's not evidence against a no-celebration-on-spend rule either way. A banner ad runs during play per one reviewer [18]. Two reviewers separately call out a forced store popup between every level [18]. One specific, single-source (C3) but vivid claim: a weekly "big prize" that "wipe[s] out all the cheats you've accumulated ... 76 drills or air blowers, gone, poof" [18] — a reward that destroys prior inventory rather than adding to it.
