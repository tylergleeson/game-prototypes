# Gate Escape — unique value proposition and store positioning

Built from the three teardowns and the ledger. The category parent (Color Block Jam) wins on a free-routing verb and an obstacle catalogue, then loses its players to a timer, ads and a fail offer. The genre original (Unblock Me) has held 4.8★ for 17 years on one honest number per puzzle — a Record against a minimum — and loses stars only to ad cadence. The calm clone (Block Game NO TIMER) proves the demand for "no timer" and then breaks its own promise with an opaque move budget and a store pop-up.

## The UVP, one paragraph

Gate Escape is the calm, honest version of the drag-the-block-to-its-gate puzzle. Every level is generated with a solver that proves it is solvable and computes the shortest solution, so the par you see is a fact and three stars can only mean you found it. The move limit is printed as what it is — par plus two — never a hidden budget. There is no timer, no lives, no ad between levels and no ad that is the only way past a board, and the bot checks all of that on every build before it ships. One drag moves a block any distance, around corners, so the puzzle is route-finding rather than shunting, and every colour carries its own symbol so four inks stay legible to everyone. A year of daily drafts, a spoiler-free share line and a weekly survey give it a habit loop that measures one thing: did you clear a level today.

## Three ownable claims

None of the three apps can make these without changing their business model or their engine.

1. **Every star is a proof.** Par is computed by the solver that built the level; 3★ only at par; the limit is par + 2 and says so. (Unblock Me's Record makes no optimality claim; Color Block Jam has no move economy at all; NO TIMER shows a flat "Moves 30".)
2. **No timer, no lives, no ads between levels — verified on every build.** The playtest bot fails the build if a clock, a life or an interstitial appears; the ⓘ revision notes carry the build stamp. (NO TIMER's own description says "on time"; Color Block Jam's timer is its most complained-about mechanic; Unblock Me's only real complaint is ad cadence.)
3. **One drag, any distance, around corners — and a symbol on every colour.** Route-finding, not rail-shunting, on a board that four-colour-blind players can read. (Color Block Jam differentiates up to nine blocks by hue alone; Unblock Me is rail-locked.)
