# Gate Escape — weekly creative testing loop

The loop exists because creative velocity is the binding constraint: 70–120 variants/week
at paid scale, a 5–15% hit rate, fatigue within a week (research constraint 3). Organic
Phase A runs the same loop at 5–7 variants/week; the *shape* of the loop never changes,
only N.

## 1. Naming convention — hook × moment × format

```
b<batch>-v<nn>_<hookId>_<moment>_<format>.mp4
b01-v01_H01_m2_raw.mp4
```
- `b<batch>` two digits, `v<nn>` two digits, position in the batch.
- `<hookId>` from `hooks.md` (H01–H50; append, never renumber). A new hook not yet in the
  library gets the next free ID *before* the file is rendered.
- `<moment>` `m1`–`m4` (showcase moments), `t01`–`t14` (tour chapters), `ugc` (founder
  footage), `cap` (a new capture recipe — describe it in the manifest).
- `<format>` `raw` (gameplay only), `solve` (can-you-solve-it), `pov`, `tut` (tutorial),
  `ugc` (hands-on-phone), `duet`, `asmr` (satisfying), `play` (playable companion).
- The manifest (`batch-NN/manifest.json`) is the source of truth for what a name means;
  `batch-table.md` is generated by the script.

A variant differs from its parent on **exactly one axis** wherever possible, so a verdict
is attributable ("1% Better Each Video", `02_algorithm`: change one thing, read the
result).

## 2. Weekly rhythm

| day | action |
|---|---|
| Mon | Founder pastes last week's 72-h numbers into `batch-NN/perf.json`; worker runs the verdicts (§3) |
| Mon | Worker writes `batch-(NN+1)/manifest.json`: kills dropped, winners mutated, 1 new-format test per 3 (3-2-1 Strategy) |
| Mon–Tue | `node tools/tiktok-batch.mjs --spec …` → review stills (sound-off check) → post schedule set |
| Tue–Sun | one post per day (Phase A); Spark Ads (Phase C) launched Tue, read Sun |
| Sun | quick read: any post > 2× the batch median gets its comments mined for the next hooks |

## 3. The verdict rule

**Organic (Phase A/B) — attention verdicts, not install verdicts.**
- Read at **72 h** and only when the post has **≥ 500 views** (**assumption**: below that
  the hold % is noise; raise this floor as the account grows).
- Metrics, in priority order: 3-second hold % → completion % → shares → comments →
  profile visits → link clicks.
- **Kill:** 3-s hold below the batch median **and** completion below median.
- **Mutate:** 3-s hold ≥ median (keep the axis that is working; change one other axis).
- **Winner:** 3-s hold ≥ **1.5× the batch median** with shares > 0, **or** views ≥ 3× the
  batch median (it graduated a TikTok round on its own). A winner is re-tested with two
  single-axis mutations in the next batch; if at least one mutation also clears the median
  the *family* (moment × format) is confirmed.
- The batch median is recomputed each batch; "winner" is always relative to our own
  posts — we adopt no external hold benchmark (none was in the research or the course).

**Paid (Phase C funded) — install verdicts.**
- **50–100 conversions per creative** before a verdict (research constraint 2); at
  casual iOS CPI > $1 that is $50–100+ per variant, which is why nothing below the
  $2,500/month floor is called a test.
- Verdict metrics: CTR (TikTok In-Feed band 0.8–2.5%), CPI vs the kill line
  (< $0.30 Supersonic / ~$0.20 Voodoo, research constraint 5), then D1 from the beacon /
  App Analytics on the campaign-link cohort.
- Days: run each variant until it reaches the conversion floor or 7 days, whichever first;
  a variant that cannot reach 50 conversions in 7 days at the capped budget is "unread",
  not "lost".

## 4. Fatigue rotation

- Expect a paid creative to fatigue within a week (research constraint 3). Rotation rule:
  any Spark Ad whose CTR drops below 60% of its own first-48-h CTR is paused and replaced
  by its best mutation; the original returns to rotation after ≥ 14 days (**assumption**
  on the exact thresholds; the direction is the research's).
- Organic posts do not fatigue the same way, but *formats* do: no more than 3 consecutive
  posts of one format.
- Keep a **library** of ≥ 20 rendered-but-unposted variants so a fatigue swap is a
  30-second job. The batch script makes this cheap: render 10, post 7, bank 3.

## 5. What "winner" means, precisely

A **winner** is a *variant*: a specific hook × moment × format file with a measured
result. A **winning family** is a moment × format pair that produced ≥ 2 winners with
different hooks. A **winning hook** is a hook that won on ≥ 2 moments. Only families and
hooks earn budget; single winners earn mutations.

## 6. How a winner becomes a Spark Ad (Phase C)

1. The post must be ≥ 7 days old with its organic engagement intact (Spark Ads boost the
   post itself; its likes/comments are real — never seed them).
2. Founder: TikTok app → the post → Ad settings → authorise, copy the Spark code; Ads
   Manager → App Installs objective → Spark Ad → paste code; destination = the App Store
   campaign link minted for that variant (`ct=tiktok-spark-b03v04`).
3. Budget $10–20/day × 5 days (**assumption**, amplification not test); one Spark Ad per
   winning family at a time.
4. Read Sun: CTR vs the In-Feed band, campaign-link downloads on Apple's side, CPI band.
   If CPI is inside the kill line, keep it running and build the playable around it; if
   not, stop and mutate — do not add budget to a loser.

## 7. Founder input format

`batch-NN/perf.json` (template `perf-template.json`): one object per variant with
`views`, `hold3s` (%), `avgWatch` (s), `completion` (%), `likes`, `comments`, `shares`,
`saves`, `profileVisits`, `linkClicks`, `installs` (leave null until Phase C), `notes`
(free text: best comment, what people asked). The worker does the rest.
